How to play

  1. Each round, one player is the Interviewer and one is the Candidate. Everyone else sits on the Panel and watches.
  2. The Interviewer sees a secret job and asks up to 5 questions. Don't say the job or give it away.
  3. The Candidate answers each question as if they already have the job, then makes one guess. You can guess early.
  4. Right guess: both players earn Offers, more for guessing early. Wrong guess: only the Interviewer scores.
  5. Roles swap every round. Most Offers at the end wins.
